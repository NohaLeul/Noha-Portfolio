Privacy policy.

This page explains what information may be collected when you contact me or complete a project agreement, why it is used, and how you can make a privacy request.

Last Updated: 21 November 2026Website owner: Noha Leul GebregiorgisAddis Ababa, Ethiopia

1. Who is responsible for your information?

This portfolio is operated by Noha Leul Gebregiorgis, an independent web developer and designer based in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. For privacy questions or requests, contact nohaleul27@gmail.com.

2. Information that may be collected

Information you type into a form is handled in your browser to prepare an email draft. The static website does not receive or store those form entries on a website server. If you choose to send the prepared email, the recipient and Google/Gmail may process the information in that email. The information may include:

  • Your name and email address.
  • Your business or company name and other business information you choose to provide.
  • Messages, inquiries, and website project details.
  • Agreement information, such as the selected arrangement, project description, agreed price, additional conditions, date, and typed name.

Please do not send sensitive personal information through the portfolio forms unless it is genuinely necessary and you have discussed it with me first.

3. How information is used

If you choose to send a prepared email, the information in it can be used by the recipient only as reasonably needed to:

  • Respond to inquiries and communicate with you.
  • Understand and discuss a website project.
  • Prepare, review, and keep a record of project agreements.
  • Provide services you request and manage the working relationship.
  • Maintain relevant business and project records.

4. Information sharing and service providers

Your personal information is not sold. The contact and client agreement forms do not post information to a website backend, database, or Netlify Forms endpoint. When you submit either form, the browser attempts to open a pre-addressed Gmail compose window containing the details you entered. The website does not send the message. You decide whether to review and send it from Gmail. If you send it, Google processes the email under Google's own terms and privacy practices.

The website loads its typography from Google Fonts. A visitor's browser may connect to Google's font service to retrieve those fonts. The portfolio code does not intentionally send form contents to Google Fonts. The site may be hosted by a provider such as Netlify after deployment; the hosting provider may process standard technical data needed to deliver the website.

Service providers may process technical or email information on systems located in other countries, depending on their infrastructure and settings. Review the providers' current terms and privacy information before use and whenever providers change.

5. Data security

Reasonable care should be taken when handling submitted information and when configuring the website and its service providers. No website, internet transmission, or electronic storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Avoid sending passwords, payment-card details, or unnecessary sensitive information through these forms.

Access to form submissions should be limited to people who need it for the stated project or business purposes. Keep account credentials private and use the security controls offered by the hosting and form providers.

6. Cookies, local storage, and analytics

The supplied portfolio code does not include an analytics service or advertising tracker. It does not intentionally set tracking cookies. The light/dark theme preference is saved in the visitor's browser using local storage so the selected appearance can be remembered on that device. Local storage is browser storage and is not itself a cookie.

If analytics, embedded services, advertising tools, or additional cookies are added in the future, this policy should be updated to explain what they do before they are enabled.

7. Third-party links

The portfolio may link to external websites and services, including social, project-hosting, or messaging platforms. Those websites operate under their own privacy policies and practices. I do not control their data handling, so please review their policies before providing information on those sites.

8. Your privacy choices and rights

Where applicable under relevant law, you may request access to personal information about you, ask for inaccurate information to be corrected, or request deletion. You may also ask questions about how information is used or request that a particular communication be stopped. Some information may need to be retained where required for legitimate business records, legal obligations, or the establishment or defence of legal claims.

To make a request, email nohaleul27@gmail.com and describe the request clearly. I may need to ask for reasonable information to confirm which records the request concerns. I will consider and respond to requests in light of applicable requirements.

10. Applicable privacy principles and updates

This policy is intended to explain the website's current data practices in clear terms. Personal data handling in Ethiopia is addressed by the Personal Data Protection Proclamation No. 1321/2024. This page is not a legal opinion and does not guarantee compliance; the actual setup, provider terms, data flows, and legal obligations should be reviewed before the website is used to collect personal information.

The policy may be updated when the website, its forms, service providers, or applicable requirements change. The latest version and its update date will be published on this page.

Official reference: Ethiopian Ministry of Justice — Personal Data Protection Proclamation.
