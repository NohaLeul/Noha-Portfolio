WORKING TOGETHER · PROJECT TERMS

Client contracts.

A clear outline of how website projects are explored, reviewed, purchased, and managed. Please read these terms and complete the agreement details below so both sides have a written record of the arrangement.

Prepared by Noha Leul GebregiorgisWeb Developer & DesignerLast reviewed: 21 November 2026

1. Introduction

This document sets out the proposed terms for website design and development between Noha Leul Gebregiorgis (the “Developer”) and the person or business identified in the agreement form (the “Client”). It is intended to make the project scope, review process, payment arrangement, responsibilities, and handover expectations clear before the parties proceed.

The completed project-specific agreement, including any additional conditions agreed in writing, should be reviewed by both parties. If a term needs to be changed, discuss and record the change before relying on it.

Important: This is a general website-project template, not legal advice. If either party is under 18, a parent or legal guardian should be involved and the parties should obtain appropriate local legal advice before entering into a paid arrangement. Consider having an Ethiopian legal professional review the final terms.

2. Website development process

  • The Developer first researches the Client's business and discusses its goals, audience, services, and website needs.
  • The Developer prepares and develops a complete website based on the information and project scope available.
  • When a review version is ready, the Developer shares a preview link with the Client.
  • The Client may review the preview and communicate reasonable revisions or corrections.
  • The Client is given reasonable time to consider whether to purchase the website or proceed with a managed arrangement. The Client is not pressured to purchase.

3. Free-first policy

No initial website-development payment is required to begin the free-first review process. The Client can inspect the completed website through the preview link before deciding whether to purchase or enter a managed website arrangement.

If the Client decides not to proceed, no website development fee is charged for the free-first preview.

Until the agreed purchase price has been paid in full or a separate written managed arrangement has been agreed, the unpaid website and its source code remain the Developer's work. The Client may not publish, deploy, reuse, copy, sell, transfer, or claim ownership of the unpaid website or its source code without the Developer's written permission.

4. Revisions and project scope

During the review process, the Client may request reasonable design and content revisions that relate to the original project scope. Requests should be specific and communicated clearly, preferably in one organized message or document.

Major new features, additional pages, substantial changes in direction, integrations, or work outside the original scope may require a revised timeline, additional fee, or separate written agreement. The Developer will explain any proposed change before carrying it out.

5. Payment and ownership

Option 1 — One-time website purchase

  • The Client pays the one-time price agreed in the project agreement.
  • After full payment, the Developer hands over the website and the deliverables specifically agreed for transfer.
  • Ongoing maintenance, hosting, domain registration or renewal, and future updates are not automatically included.
  • Any additional support or services must be discussed and agreed separately, including their fees and responsibilities.

Option 2 — Managed website agreement

  • The Client pays the monthly or yearly fee stated in the project agreement.
  • The Developer retains control of the website and provides only the maintenance and management services specified in that agreement.
  • The written arrangement must state the payment schedule, included services, each party's responsibilities, and cancellation conditions.
  • Ownership, access, and use of the website remain subject to the managed arrangement and any separate written terms.

Prices, payment methods, deadlines, hosting arrangements, and any deliverables not listed here must be confirmed in the project-specific agreement. No price or service is implied if it has not been agreed.

6. Client responsibilities

The Client agrees to:

  • Provide accurate and current business information and project instructions.
  • Supply logos, images, text, contact details, and other materials needed for the project.
  • Review website previews and provide clear feedback within a reasonable time.
  • Ensure that they own or have permission to use all materials they provide, including images, logos, text, and other content.
  • Make payments according to the selected arrangement and agreed schedule.

7. Website delivery and handover

Delivery and handover take place according to the selected payment arrangement and after the required payment conditions have been fulfilled. For a one-time purchase, handover follows full payment. For a managed website, access, control, maintenance, and ongoing responsibilities follow the written managed agreement.

The parties should identify any specific files, credentials, domain arrangements, hosting responsibilities, or other deliverables to be handed over in the project description or additional conditions.

8. Cancellation and disagreements

Either party may raise a concern or request cancellation by communicating clearly and respectfully in writing. The parties should first discuss the issue, identify the relevant project terms, and try to reach a practical resolution in good faith.

If the Client declines the free-first preview before purchasing, no website development fee is charged and the restrictions in the free-first section continue to apply. If a paid purchase or managed arrangement has already begun, cancellation, outstanding payments, completed work, and any handover or refund questions should be resolved according to the specific written agreement and applicable law.

Neither party should assume that a cancellation, refund, or transfer term exists unless it is stated in the project agreement or required by applicable law. If direct discussion does not resolve a disagreement, either party may seek independent advice or use an appropriate dispute-resolution process.

10. Full Website Ownership Transfer and Developer Access Termination

Upon receiving the full agreed purchase payment, the Developer agrees to transfer ownership and administrative control of the completed website to the Client.

The transfer includes the website's source code, project files, agreed design assets, hosting project, domain management (where applicable), and other agreed deliverables.

After the successful handover:

1. The Client becomes responsible for managing, maintaining, hosting, and updating the website.

2. The Developer will relinquish administrative access to the transferred website, hosting account, repository, and other transferred services.

3. The Developer will not access, modify, monitor, or manage the website without the Client's explicit permission.

4. The Developer will not retain access to the Client's private business information, customer information, website submissions, or confidential credentials.

5. The Client is responsible for future hosting fees, domain renewals, maintenance, and other third-party service costs.

6. Any future assistance requested from the Developer will require a separate agreement.

The Developer may retain only those non-confidential portfolio materials that the Client has explicitly permitted the Developer to display.

The ownership transfer will be considered complete once the Client has received the agreed deliverables, confirmed access to the transferred accounts, and accepted the handover.
