01 / ABOUTTHE PERSON BEHIND THE PIXELS

HELLO, I'M NOHA

Curiosity turned into a craft.

EST. AGE 9
HTML + CSS

My interest in web development began when I was around nine years old. I started learning HTML and CSS, exploring websites, and wondering how they were put together. Before long, I wanted to create one myself — so I tried, enjoyed it, and kept learning.

Today, I build websites with one thought in mind: the experience belongs to the people using it. I want the first few seconds to spark curiosity, and every moment after that to feel clear, smooth, and worthwhile.

I'm an independent web developer and designer based in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia, focused on helping service-based businesses build a strong and professional online presence.
